# Privacy Policy - Photos
Last Updated: 2026-10-24

## 1. Principles
Photos is an offline-first gallery and media organization application built by Arinara Network. We believe personal memories and photographs should remain completely private to the device owner. Photos does not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal photos, media files, EXIF metadata, or search queries to remote servers.

## 2. On-Device Media Processing
All indexing, thumbnail generation, EXIF parsing, album sorting, and tag categorization occur exclusively on your local device hardware. No image bytes or visual features are uploaded to external cloud endpoints.

## 3. Storage and Permissions
Photos requests Android media storage permissions (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on supported Android versions) solely to display and manage the photos already present on your device. Photos never accesses media outside the locations you browse.

## 4. Analytics and Telemetry
Photos operates with zero third-party telemetry, tracking SDKs, or advertising identifiers.

## 5. Contact
For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact privacy@arinara.network.
